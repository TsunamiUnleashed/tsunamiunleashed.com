Use this question set in **pre-gathering contexts** — conversations with people who are not yet part of a Jesus Community. The flow follows the Prodigal Son story (Luke 15:11–32) and mirrors the Three Verses.

This is **relational**, not head-knowledge. The questions draw the person toward their own experience of God, not toward facts about Him.

## The Flow

### See — God's Love
**"What is a father like?"** or **"What is a good father like?"**

*(Mirrors John 3:16 — God so loved.)*

### Shift — Becoming His Child
**"What is our relationship to a father — or to a good father?"**

*(Mirrors John 1:12–13 — receiving Him, becoming children of God.)*

### Sift — Loving God First
**"If we have a good father and know that relationship, what do we do?"**

*(Mirrors Matthew 22:37–40 / Luke 10:27 — loving God is first.)*
