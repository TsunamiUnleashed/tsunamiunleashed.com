The Three Verses are the Tier 1 message backbone of Tsunami Unleashed. They tell the essential journey of life with Jesus in three steps: **See** God's love, **Shift** into His family, **Sift** everything else through love for Him.

## The Verses

{{< verse ref="John 3:16" >}}
For God so loved the world that he gave his one and only Son, that whoever believes in him shall not perish but have eternal life.
{{< /verse >}}

**See** — God loves you and gave everything for you.

{{< verse ref="John 1:12-13" >}}
Yet to all who did receive him, to those who believed in his name, he gave the right to become children of God — children born not of natural descent, nor of human decision or a husband's will, but born of God.
{{< /verse >}}

**Shift** — You can receive Him and become His child.

{{< verse ref="Matthew 22:37-40" >}}
Jesus replied: "Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind." This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: "Love your neighbor as yourself." All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments.
{{< /verse >}}

**Sift** — Loving God is first. Everything else flows from there.
